⚖️ Pre-Specified Eligibility Criteria (PRISMA 2020)

Sysrevai's algorithmic screening engine evaluates every retrieved article against these inclusion and exclusion rules, assigning each study an automated eligibility classification with explicit exclusion rationales.

✔ Inclusion Criteria 5 rules

  • Human clinical investigations (adult population ≥ 18 years)
  • Strictly one of 9 empirical designs (RCT, Non-RCT, Cohort, Case-Control, Cross-Sectional, Diagnostic, Case Series/Report, Qualitative, Observational)
  • Evaluates targeted intervention or clinical exposure against comparator/control
  • Reports empirical primary outcomes, findings, or qualitative analyses
  • Peer-reviewed primary research published in scholarly biomedical journals

✖ Exclusion Criteria 5 rules

  • Pre-clinical animal models, in-vitro studies, or tissue culture experiments
  • Narrative reviews, secondary systematic reviews, editorials, commentaries, letters, or protocols
  • Non-empirical opinion pieces, meeting summaries, or conference abstracts without data
  • Duplicate cohort publications or redundant secondary data reports
  • Studies lacking primary clinical data or missing methodological details
