Scientific & Clinical AI Advisory

Methodological Protocol & Algorithmic Synthesis Guidance

SysRev AI is an auxiliary evidence synthesis platform designed to streamline systematic review workflows according to PRISMA 2020 protocol standards. It is intended exclusively for academic, research, and methodological assistance and does not constitute a medical device or a substitute for expert clinical judgment.

⚠️ Probabilistic Generative AI Notice: Underlying artificial intelligence models operate probabilistically. While SysRev AI employs strict grounding, deduplication, and algorithmic verifications, automated models may occasionally omit nuances, misinterpret complex biomedical context, or generate imprecise extractions.

Mandatory Investigator Responsibilities:

  • Supervised Study Selection: All PICOS filtering, automated study classifications, and eligibility decisions require independent human verification.
  • Methodological Quality: Cochrane Risk of Bias (RoB) appraisals, domain judgements, and extracted effect sizes must be inspected against primary publications.
  • Clinical Decision-Making: Synthesized findings, forest plots, and summary manuscripts must never be deployed directly for diagnostic, therapeutic, or clinical patient management without independent clinical peer review.

By continuing to use SysRev AI, you acknowledge that you are acting as an independent investigator and assume full responsibility for the validation of all literature records, statistical pooled outcomes, and manuscript conclusions.

⚖️ Pre-Specified Eligibility Criteria (PRISMA 2020)

Sysrevai's algorithmic screening engine evaluates every retrieved article against these inclusion and exclusion rules, assigning each study an automated eligibility classification with explicit exclusion rationales.

✔ Inclusion Criteria 5 rules

  • Human clinical investigations (adult population ≥ 18 years)
  • Strictly one of 9 empirical designs (RCT, Non-RCT, Cohort, Case-Control, Cross-Sectional, Diagnostic, Case Series/Report, Qualitative, Observational)
  • Evaluates targeted intervention or clinical exposure against comparator/control
  • Reports empirical primary outcomes, findings, or qualitative analyses
  • Peer-reviewed primary research published in scholarly biomedical journals

✖ Exclusion Criteria 5 rules

  • Pre-clinical animal models, in-vitro studies, or tissue culture experiments
  • Narrative reviews, secondary systematic reviews, editorials, commentaries, letters, or protocols
  • Non-empirical opinion pieces, meeting summaries, or conference abstracts without data
  • Duplicate cohort publications or redundant secondary data reports
  • Studies lacking primary clinical data or missing methodological details
